{%- comment -%} The collaborations map. Geometry — coastlines, graticule, the arcs from Madrid and every projected point — is baked into _data/network_geometry.yml by bin/bake-map.mjs and simply read here. Nothing is projected in the browser: no d3, no topojson, no world-atlas download, no third-party request of any kind. Labels, institutions and member names are read live from network.yml and people.yml, so editing those needs no bake. Two views are emitted and CSS shows one: a desktop composition with the Europe enlargement beside the world, and a phone composition with it stacked underneath. They share one copy of the geometry through , because each is an affine transform of the other — which is why the coastline data appears once in the page rather than twice. Six of the nine institutions are European, too close together to tell apart at world scale. They are drawn twice: once in the world, muted and aria-hidden with a transparent hit circle so hover still works, and once in the enlargement, where they take focus. Only one copy is focusable, or a keyboard user would get fifteen stops for nine institutions. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign net = site.data.network -%} {%- assign geo = site.data.network_geometry -%} {%- assign all_people = site.data.people.researchers_madrid | concat: site.data.people.students_madrid | concat: site.data.people.international_collaborators | concat: site.data.people.visitors -%} {%- assign uid = include.uid | default: "nm" -%}
