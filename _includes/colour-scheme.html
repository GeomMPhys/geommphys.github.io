{%- comment -%} The header colour-scheme control, built to the spec in top_bar.md (drawn as 6a in "Job 3 - Night toggle.dc.html"): three 15 px glyphs at the far right of the header — a screen for Auto, a sun for Light, a crescent for Dark. Three states, not two. Auto is the site's own default, so a two-way toggle would leave a reader no way back to "follow my system" once they touched it. `aria-pressed` on three buttons in one role="group" is deliberate: it states the current state without claiming the group is a radio set, which is what the ringed active glyph shows visually. The script runs here rather than deferred so the pressed state is written the moment the buttons are parsed; a deferred copy would leave the wrong glyph ringed for a frame.
